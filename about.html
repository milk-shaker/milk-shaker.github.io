No mother should have to choose between pain and caregiving.

We are female engineers — and the mothers in our own lives kept running into clogged ducts and mastitis, shocked that nothing worked. So we listened to more moms, partnered with FemFirst Health, and set out to build something that does good.

By women, for women.

We apply our mechanical engineering backgrounds to prototyping the mechanisms that bring the specialist massage home.

We are partnered with gynecologists, women’s health researchers, and certified physical and occupational therapists from FemFirst Health.

Their insight keeps MilkShaker grounded in the real needs of breastfeeding women.
