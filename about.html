We believe no mother should have to choose between pain and caregiving.

We are a team of women biomedical engineers and lactation specialists—and the mothers in our own lives kept running into clogged ducts and mastitis, shocked that nothing worked. So we listened to more moms, partnered with maternal health clinics, and set out to build something that does good.

  • Lactation expert-informed
  • Clinically partnered
  • Mom-tested
  • Female engineer-created

We are partnered with OB/GYNs, pediatricians, women’s health researchers, and certified physical and occupational therapists.

Their insight keeps MilkShaker grounded in the real needs of breastfeeding women.
