Our story

No mother should have to choose between pain and caregiving.

We are a team of female engineers driven by a deep commitment to women’s health and united by our personal connections to women who have faced mastitis and other breastfeeding challenges.

The problem

Common, painful, and badly served.

How often it happens

Mastitis affects up to 1 in 3 breastfeeding mothers. It occurs when a clogged milk duct — affecting up to 2 in 3 — becomes infected, causing weeks of intense pain and stress for the mother and restricting milk production for the child. This happens because of poor lymphatic drainage from postpartum posture changes.

What the options are

Many mothers resort to at-home remedies or neglect the issue, enduring high fevers, painful breasts, and difficulties caring for their children. Certified professional manual lymphatic drainage is the only clinically proven and Academy of Breastfeeding Medicine-recommended treatment, but it remains inaccessible and expensive.

Who we work with

We are partnered with gynecologists, fellow engineers, women’s health researchers, and certified physical and occupational therapists from FemFirst Health.

Their expert insights ensure that MilkShaker effectively and safely meets the real-world needs of breastfeeding women.

Our team

By women, for women.

Our creative team applies our mechanical engineering backgrounds to the women’s health space, developing and prototyping the mechanisms needed to replicate the complex massage treatment for mastitis. We come from various experiences within the field, all passionate about the MilkShaker mission to expand women’s health.
