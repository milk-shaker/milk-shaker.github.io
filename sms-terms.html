SMS Support Terms

Last updated 4 September 2026

These terms cover MilkShaker’s free text-message support service. The short version appears next to the button on our opt-in page; this is the full version.

1. What the service is

MilkShaker Inc. offers free one-to-one SMS support for breastfeeding mothers dealing with clogged ducts and mastitis-related discomfort. The service is staffed by a Certified Lactation Counselor. Messages provide general breastfeeding support, education and encouragement.

2. What the service is not

We do not diagnose or treat anything. Nothing we send is medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a prescription, and texting us does not create a clinician–patient relationship. We cannot examine you. Where something appears to need a clinician we will say so and refer you to one.

We are not an emergency service. Messages are answered by a person, not continuously, and there may be a delay. If you have an emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room rather than waiting for a reply. If you have a fever, spreading redness, worsening pain, or symptoms that have not improved in about 24 hours, contact your physician, midwife or lactation consultant.

3. How you join

You join by sending START, JOIN or HI as a text message to (917) 808-5179 from the mobile number you want us to reply to. You may have found that number on a card or QR code handed out at a maternal health or new-parent community event, or on our page at themilkshaker.com/text.

Sending that message is your consent to receive recurring text messages from us at that number. Because you start the conversation, we only ever message a number that has messaged us first. Consent is not a condition of any purchase — the service is free and there is nothing to buy.

4. Messages, frequency and cost

  • Program: MilkShaker breastfeeding text support.
  • Message frequency varies. This is a conversation, not a broadcast, so how many messages you get depends on how much you write to us.
  • Message and data rates may apply. The service is free from us; what your carrier charges for texts is between you and your carrier.
  • Available to mobile numbers in the United States. You must be 18 or older, and the account holder for or an authorised user of the number you text from.
  • Supported on most US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages, and delivery depends on your carrier’s network rather than on us.

5. Stopping

Reply STOP to any message and we will stop texting you. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and nothing after that. You can rejoin whenever you like by texting START again.

6. Getting help

Reply HELP to any message, or email valentina@themilkshaker.com.

7. Text messaging is not private

SMS is not encrypted, and messages pass through your mobile carrier and ours. Anyone with access to your phone can read them. Please do not send anything you would not want read by someone else, and bear in mind that what you tell us about your health arrives over an unsecured channel. If you would rather not discuss something by text, email us instead.

8. What we do with your messages

We use your number and the content of your messages to reply to you and to run the service. We do not sell, rent or trade your number. Our Privacy Policy sets out the detail, including how long we keep things and how to ask us to delete them.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. If we change them in a way that affects what you have agreed to, we will say so by text before it takes effect. The “last updated” date above always reflects the current version.
