Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

This policy explains what MilkShaker Inc. collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It covers themilkshaker.com and our email list.

1. The short version

We collect an email address if you give us one, and anonymous statistics about which pages get read. We use them to send you the thing you asked for and to understand what is useful. We do not sell your details to anyone, and you can have them deleted by asking.

2. What we collect

If you join the list

Your first name and email address. That is all the form asks for and all we keep.

If you use the contact form

Your name, your email address and whatever you write to us.

Both forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party service that receives the submission and emails it to us. Their handling of it is governed by their own terms.

If you reach a page we no longer link to

A few older pages remain reachable by direct link and still have working forms on them. One collects name, email and whether you would reserve the product at a given price. A follow-up page also asks how a price feels to you and whether you have had mastitis or clogged ducts before, which is health information. We mention it because it is still collectable, not because we are asking for it.

When you browse

We use GoatCounter for analytics. It records which pages are viewed, roughly which country from, and which site referred you. It sets no tracking cookies and does not follow you between sites.

3. Health information

Some of what people choose to tell us is health information. We are not a healthcare provider, we do not bill insurance, and we are not a HIPAA covered entity, so HIPAA does not apply to anything you send us. The protections you would expect from a doctor’s office do not automatically apply here.

What does apply: we use it only to reply to you, we do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we share it only with the services in section 5. Email is also not an encrypted channel, so please bear that in mind before writing something sensitive.

4. Why we collect it

  • To send the list emails you asked for.
  • To answer messages sent through the contact form.
  • To see which pages are useful, in aggregate.

We do not use your details for advertising and we do not build profiles on people. Joining the list does not sign you up to anything else.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the services that make the site work:

  • FormSubmit — delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • GoatCounter — aggregate analytics.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this site and, like any host, sees request logs.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

6. How long we keep it

[RETENTION — confirm and replace this sentence] We keep list subscriptions until you unsubscribe, and messages for as long as needed to deal with them and a reasonable period afterwards. We delete things on request.

7. What you can ask us to do

Email valentina@themilkshaker.com and we will:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete it, unless we are required to keep it;
  • take you off the list, though the unsubscribe link is faster.

We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front, and we will not treat you differently for exercising any of this.

8. Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent us something, email us and we will delete it.

9. Where we operate

We are based in the United States, and anything you send us is processed there.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change materially affects people on the list, we will say so in a list email.
