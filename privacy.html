Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

This policy explains what MilkShaker Inc. collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It covers themilkshaker.com and our text-message support service.

1. What we collect

If you text our support number

We receive your mobile number and whatever you write to us, which in practice often includes information about your health. We also keep our replies, so the conversation makes sense next time you write.

If you fill in a form on this site

Our contact form collects your name and email address. Two older pages that are no longer linked from the site menus, but still work if you have the link, collect a little more: our preorder page collects name, email and whether you would reserve at a given price, and a follow-up page collects name, email, how a price feels to you, and whether you have had mastitis or clogged ducts before.

Those forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party service that takes the submission and emails it to us. Their handling of it is governed by their own terms.

When you browse

We use GoatCounter for analytics. It records which pages are viewed, roughly where in the world from, and which site referred you. It does not set tracking cookies and does not follow you between sites.

2. Health information

Some of what people tell us is health information, and we want to be plain about how it is handled. We are not a healthcare provider, we do not bill insurance, and we are not a HIPAA covered entity, so HIPAA does not apply to these messages. That means the protections you might expect from a doctor’s office do not automatically apply here.

What we do instead: we use what you tell us only to support you, we do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the services listed in section 4. Text messaging is also not a secure channel, which is covered in our SMS Terms.

3. Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and provide the support you asked for.
  • To answer enquiries sent through the contact form.
  • To understand which pages are useful, in aggregate.

We do not use your phone number or email for advertising, and we do not add people who text the support line to any marketing list. If we ever want to send you something promotional, we will ask separately first.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the services that make the site work:

  • Mobile carriers — unavoidably, in order to deliver a text message.
  • FormSubmit — delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • GoatCounter — aggregate site analytics.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this site and, like any web host, sees request logs.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

[RETENTION — confirm and replace this sentence] We keep support conversations and form submissions for as long as needed to provide the service and for a reasonable period afterwards, and delete them on request.

6. What you can ask us to do

Email valentina@themilkshaker.com and we will:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it, unless we are required to keep it;
  • stop texting you — though replying STOP is faster.

We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of this.

7. Children

This site and the text service are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent us something, email us and we will delete it.

8. Where we operate

We are based in the United States and the text service is available only to US mobile numbers. If you are outside the US, anything you send us is processed in the US.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change materially affects people using the text service, we will say so by text.
