MilkShaker is a nursing bra with massaging modules inside the cups, designed with lactation consultants and women’s health physicians for the clogged ducts and breast pain that send new parents back to your clinic — often at the point where they stop breastfeeding altogether.

We are building the first units now, and we are choosing the clinical and education partners we launch with. If your patients or your members would benefit, we would like to talk.

Three ways we work together

Each one starts with a short, non-binding letter of intent, so nobody is committing money before there is a product to buy.

  • Clinics and hospital systems

    Supply at a partner price, in bulk, from our first production run, with intent agreed now and quantities confirmed closer to launch. Your practitioners see the product before anyone else and tell us what to change while changing it is still cheap.

  • Education organisations

    Sessions for labour, delivery and postpartum staff on clogged ducts, mastitis and the mechanics of relief, run with our lactation consultant advisors. The teaching stands on its own, whether or not a single unit is ever bought.

  • Clinical advisory board

    A small group of maternity and lactation clinicians who meet with us for an hour a quarter and shape what we test and how. It is an advisory seat, not a commercial one: no equity, no obligation, and you can step off at any point.

Who is already around the table

Our team, advisors and partner organisations come from across clinical care, engineering and perinatal education — including a postpartum clinic partner at Fem First Health, a physician advisor at NYU Langone Health and a paediatrician advisor at UCSF.
