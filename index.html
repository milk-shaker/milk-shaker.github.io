What this is

  • A real person, not a bot. Your messages go to a Certified Lactation Counselor who answers you directly.
  • Support, education and encouragement. Help thinking through what you are feeling, what tends to help, and what to keep an eye on. Plain language, no judgement, at whatever hour you are awake.
  • Free, and staying free. There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for beyond the text. Your carrier’s usual message and data rates are the only cost.
  • Not a diagnosis, and not treatment. We cannot examine you or tell you what you have. When something needs a clinician, we say so and point you to one.

When to call a clinician instead

Texting us is not a substitute for medical care, and we are not an emergency service. Please contact your physician, midwife or lactation consultant if you have:

  • a fever, or you feel flu-like and unwell
  • redness that is spreading, or a hard area that keeps growing
  • breast pain that is getting worse rather than better
  • symptoms that have not improved in about 24 hours

If this is an emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room. Do not wait for a text back.
