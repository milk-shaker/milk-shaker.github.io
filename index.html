OUR STORY

No mother should have to choose between pain and caregiving

We are a team of five female engineers driven by a deep commitment to women's health and united by our personal connections to women who have faced mastitis and other breastfeeding challenges.

Our product development process has been supported by invaluable partnerships with gynecologists, fellow engineers, women's health researchers, and most notably, certified physical therapists from FemFirst Health . Their expert insights ensure that MilkShaker effectively and safely meets the real-world needs of breastfeeding women.

An alarming problem faced by at least 1 in 10 breastfeeding mothers is mastitis, a condition that occurs when a clogged milk duct – affecting 1 in 5 – becomes infected, causing intense pain and stress for the mother and restricting milk production for the child. This occurs due to poor lymphatic drainage from postpartum posture changes.

Many mothers resort to at-home remedies or neglect the issue, enduring high fevers, painful breasts, and difficulties caring for their children. Certified physical therapy manual lymphatic drainage (MLD) is the only proven treatment, but remains inaccessible and expensive.

Our novel design integrates a fully automatic mechanical massage system into a comfortable, supportive vest-like garment. Developed in partnership with NYC’s FemFirst Health clinic, MilkShaker replicates massages performed by certified physical therapists—the most effective treatment for mastitis but difficult to access due to limited certifications.

As the only device of its kind, MilkShaker brings professional care into the home, prioritizing function, accessibility, mobility, and safety. With funding from Startup Columbia and others, we envision MilkShaker as standard breastfeeding equipment, HSA-eligible and covered under the Affordable Care Act. Our product gives all mothers a new opportunity to alleviate an inevitable breastfeeding pain.

Our Product

MilkShaker

An automated, physical therapist-backed wearable to prevent and treat mastitis in breastfeeding women.

Automated Professional Massage

The mechanical system performs a continuous, 30-minute professional lymphatic drainage massage based on quantified pressure, force, and direction from certified PTs at FemFirst.

Portable & Hands Free

Our rechargeable technology allows for the mobility of new mothers. With 10 massages per overnight charge, new mothers can perform daily tasks without sacrificing their health.

Washable, Removable Technology

Our mechanical massager sits within the breast pockets of the wearable vest-like garment, allowing for complete electronics removal for easy machine washing and part replacement.

Direct Pushing Massage

Certified lymphatic massages involve a light, pushing motion unlike deep massagers. Our proprietary mechanism is the only option on the market providing this science-backed massage.

Breast-Specific

MilkShaker, both preventative and a treatment device, targets the root cause of mastitis via light, directional massage over and around the breast, proven to stimulate lymphatic fluid flow.

Medical-Grade Safety

MilkShaker adheres to medical biocompatibility standards with skin-safe materials and an enclosed electronic housing, ensuring safe, comfortable use for all breastfeeding mothers.
