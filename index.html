09Questions

The things people ask us first.

Is it safe to wear while I’m nursing or pumping?

Yes. It’s built to be the nursing bra you were going to wear anyway, and it works for both nursing and pumping. The modules run a single session at a time, easing discomfort and supporting milk flow while you have it on. As with anything you wear, stop if something doesn’t feel right, and check with your clinician or lactation consultant if you’re unsure.

How do I clean it?

Take the two modules and the battery out — they lift straight out, so there’s nothing to clean around — and the bra goes in the wash. Wipe the modules with a damp cloth and don’t submerge them.

What size should I get?

MilkShaker comes in XS through XL. If you normally wear a nursing bra, order the size you already wear in that. There will be a custom sizing step before anything ships, so you won’t have to guess.

Do I need a prescription?

No. MilkShaker is a device sold directly, so you’ll be able to get it without a prescription or a referral. If you’re already working with a lactation consultant or a provider, it’s worth telling them you’re using it so it fits alongside whatever else they’ve suggested.

When does it ship?

Spring 2027. We’re not taking orders or payment before then, and the list is how you hear first — including if that date moves.

Is joining the list a commitment?

None at all. There’s no payment, no deposit and no obligation to buy anything later. You can leave with the unsubscribe link in any email.
