09Questions

The things people ask us first.

Is it safe to wear while I’m nursing or pumping?

Yes. It’s built to be the nursing bra you were going to wear anyway, and it works for both nursing and pumping. The modules run a single session at a time, easing discomfort and supporting milk flow while you have it on. As with anything you wear, stop if something doesn’t feel right, and check with your clinician or lactation consultant if you’re unsure.

How do I clean it?

Take the two modules and the battery out — they lift straight out, so there’s nothing to clean around — and the bra goes in the wash. Wipe the modules with a damp cloth and don’t submerge them.

What size should I get?

MilkShaker comes in XS through XL. If you normally wear a nursing bra, the size you already wear in that is the one to go for. When it’s ready there will be a sizing step before you order, so you don’t have to guess.

Do I need a prescription?

No. It’s a device you’ll be able to buy directly, without a prescription or a referral. If you’re already working with a lactation consultant or a provider, it’s worth telling them you’re using it so it fits alongside whatever else they’ve suggested.
